Privacy Policy for Google Maps CSV Exporter

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

Google Maps CSV Exporter is a Chrome extension designed to help users locally export visible business search results from Google Maps into a CSV file.

Data Collection and Use

This extension does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personally identifiable information or user data. All processing occurs locally within your web browser on your own device.

When you click to export visible results on Google Maps, the extension reads the visible text and links on the active tab transiently in browser memory to generate a local CSV download. No data is ever sent to external servers or third parties.
